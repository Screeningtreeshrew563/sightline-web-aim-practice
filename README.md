## 🎯 Features

This software includes specific modes for player improvement:

- Flick shots: Click targets that appear at random points on the screen. This builds muscle memory for quick movements.
- Tracking: Follow a moving target with your cursor. This improves control during sustained fire.
- Reaction speed: Click targets the moment they change color. This measures your hardware latency and your personal response time.
- Target switching: Engage multiple targets in sequence. This simulates real combat scenarios where you face more than one enemy.

## 🖱️ Improving your aim

Consistency matters. Practice for fifteen minutes each day. Do not overwork your hand. Take breaks if you feel strain. 

Adjust your mouse settings carefully. Keep your sensitivity low if you play games that require precision. Keep your sensitivity high if you play games that require fast movement. Use the settings menu inside the software to save your profile.

## ❓ Frequently asked questions

Do I need a special mouse?
No. Any optical or laser mouse works well. High-quality sensors provide better results.

Is there a cost?
No. This tool is free to use.

How do I save my progress?
The browser uses local storage to keep your high scores. Do not clear your browser cache if you want to keep your historical data.

Will this work on a laptop?
Yes. It works on laptops, but use an external mouse for better accuracy.

Can I change the screen size?
Yes. Press F11 on your keyboard to enter full-screen mode. This removes browser menus and provides a clean view.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

If the screen looks blurry:
Check your monitor settings. Ensure the resolution matches your screen.

If the mouse feels sluggish:
Turn off mouse acceleration in your Windows settings. Search for "Mouse settings" in the Windows start menu. Select "Additional mouse options" and uncheck "Enhance pointer precision."

If the page fails to load:
Check your internet connection. Refresh the page. You may also clear your browser history and try again.
